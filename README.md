## Work

| | Project | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | **[JithuBrowser](https://github.com/Jitheswar/JithuBrowser)** | A privacy-focused browser built on official Chromium. 23 patches; it passes all 35 tests of its privacy suite on Linux, where stock Chrome fails 31. [Live demo](https://jitheswar.github.io/#work): what a page can see about you. |

| 04 | **[Diabetic retinopathy screening](https://github.com/Jitheswar/sih26038)** | Screening for rural India (SIH 2026) that checks its own explanations against lesion evidence and sends unclear cases to a person. Validation sensitivity 0.982, specificity 0.917. |
| 05 | **[Agent spend policy engine](https://github.com/Jitheswar/agent-spend-policy-engine)** | Decides whether an AI agent may spend money before it pays. Real payments on Algorand testnet, with an audit log anyone can verify. [Live demo](https://jitheswar.github.io/#work): tamper with a record, then verify. |
| 06 | **[Ayur-Ai](https://github.com/Jitheswar/Ayur-Ai)** | Identifies Ayurvedic leaves from a photo and looks up their uses. Fully offline, no LLM. 98.6% cross-validated accuracy. |

## How I work

I'd rather publish a failed result than a number nobody can reproduce. My projects keep a written record of what was tried and dropped, not just what shipped. [`Ayur-Ai/experiments/journal.md`](https://github.com/Jitheswar/Ayur-Ai/blob/main/experiments/journal.md) logs three optimizations that didn't work, and `sih26038` records the goals it didn't meet.
